Project proposal

Music in Your Hands

I propose creating a minimal gesture-controlled music player using ml5.js and p5.js. The project explores how hand movements can make everyday music playback more playful while reducing the need to reach for a mouse or keyboard.

Users will select local audio files and control playback through webcam gestures. Holding an open palm will play or pause music, swiping horizontally will change tracks, and pinching while moving vertically will adjust volume. The interface is intended for people listening to music while drawing, making things, or working at a desk.

ml5.js HandPose will estimate hand landmarks. My code will interpret their positions and movement as playback commands, while p5.js will display a mirrored camera preview and tracking visuals. Gesture labels and a hold-progress indicator will make the system’s interpretation visible.

The first prototype will test gesture recognition, playback, and visual feedback. Command cooldowns and an enable switch will help reduce accidental activation, while standard buttons will remain available. I will evaluate task completion, unintended commands, and comfort under different lighting and hand positions, then refine the gesture rules based on observations and user feedback.
