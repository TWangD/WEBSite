Project Proposal: Music in Your Hands

I propose creating a gesture-controlled music interface using ml5.js and p5.js. Users will control YouTube Music through hand movements captured by a webcam. The project explores how everyday music playback can become more playful and intuitive without requiring a mouse or keyboard.

This interface is intended for people listening to music while drawing, making things, or working at a desk. Holding an open palm will play or pause music, swiping left or right will change tracks, and pinching while moving vertically will adjust volume. A mirrored camera preview, hand landmarks, and gesture labels will provide immediate visual feedback.

ml5.js HandPose will track hand landmarks, while my code will translate their positions and movements into commands. p5.js will display the camera and tracking visuals. A small browser extension will connect the interface to YouTube Music; this connection is currently being developed and is not yet verified.

The first prototype will test gesture recognition, playback controls, and visual feedback. Short cooldowns and a gesture-enable switch will help reduce accidental commands. I will evaluate how reliably users complete basic playback tasks under different lighting and hand positions, then refine the interface based on errors and user feedback.
