Project proposal

Music in Your Hands

I propose creating a minimal gesture-controlled music player using ml5.js and p5.js. The project explores how hand movements can make everyday music playback more playful while reducing the need to reach for a mouse or keyboard.

Users select local audio files and control playback through webcam gestures. An open palm toggles playback, horizontal swipes change tracks, and a held pinch adjusts volume as the hand moves vertically. The interface is intended for people listening to music while drawing, making things, or working at a desk.

ml5.js HandPose estimates hand landmarks. My code interprets their positions and movement as playback commands. p5.js displays a mirrored camera preview, and gesture labels, a hold indicator, and a confirmation badge show what the player detects and does.

I will evaluate task completion, unintended commands, and comfort under different lighting and hand positions. Those observations will guide changes to the gesture rules and feedback. Standard playback buttons give users another way to control the player.
